About the Portal

This portal helps restaurants, bakeries, university canteens and other food contributors list surplus food for redistribution. NGOs and community organisations can identify available donations, coordinate pickups and track the redistribution process.

Our goal: Reduce food wastage, improve donation coordination and help surplus food reach people who need it.
